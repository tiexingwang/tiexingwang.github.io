<p> I'm a <strong style="font-weight:700; font-size: 1.05em;">Research Geophysicist</strong> in the R&D team at <a href="https://www.shearwatergeo.com/" style="text-decoration: underline dotted; color:#333; font-weight: 600;">Shearwater GeoServices</a> in the United Kingdom. I hold a Ph.D. in Applied Physics and have over five years of experience applying <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">AI</span> and <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">scientific machine learning</span> to real-world geophysical challenges. </p> 

<p> My work focuses on bridging <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">physics-based modeling</span> with <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">machine learning</span> to develop robust solutions for seismic inversion and data processing. At Shearwater, I contribute to R&D in: </p> <ul> <li><span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">GenAI + physics-powered sparse data interpolation</span></li> <li><span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">Physics-informed ML</span> for multiple elimination</li> <li><span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">Marine vibroseis</span> technology and sustainable source design</li> <li>Development of <strong>Reveal</strong>, the industry-leading seismic data processing software</li> </ul> 

<p> My long-term goal is to create a <strong>physics-aware deep learning framework</strong> that is both scientifically rigorous and impactful across the energy sector—from offshore wind to carbon storage. </p> 

<p> Beyond technical research, I actively contribute to the geoscience community. I currently serve as <strong>Vice President</strong> of the <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/eage-local-chapter-london-450713139/" style="text-decoration: underline dotted; color:#333; font-weight: 600;">EAGE Local Chapter London</a> and as a committee member of the <a href="https://eage.org/communities/young-professionals/" style="text-decoration: underline dotted; color:#333; font-weight: 600;">EAGE Young Professionals</a>. 

<p> I also supervise bachelor thesis projects in the Computer Science Department at <a href="https://www.tudelft.nl/" style="text-decoration: underline dotted; color:#333; font-weight: 600;">Delft University of Technology</a>. </p> 

<p> Through these roles, I help promote energy innovation, support early-career geoscientists, and advance interdisciplinary dialogue within our field. </p> <p> I'm always open to new ideas, interdisciplinary collaboration, and partnerships at the intersection of <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">AI</span>, <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">earth sciences</span>, and <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">sustainability</span>. If you'd like to connect, chat over coffee, or explore potential collaborations, feel free to reach out using the links below. </p>
