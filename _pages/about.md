<p>
  I'm a <strong style="font-weight:700; font-size: 1.05em;">Project Research Geophysicist</strong> on the R&D team at 
  <a href="https://www.shearwatergeo.com/" style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-style: dotted; text-decoration-color: rgba(0,0,0,0.3); font-weight: 600;">Shearwater GeoServices</a> in the United Kingdom. 
  I hold a Ph.D. in applied physics and have almost six years of experience applying 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">scientific machine learning</span>, 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">generative AI</span>, and 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">signal processing</span> to real-world challenges in subsurface imaging.
</p>

<p>
  My research focuses on bridging <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">physics-based modeling</span> with 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">machine learning</span>, particularly in developing 
  <strong>self-supervised, physics-informed frameworks</strong>. 
  These frameworks aim to serve as robust solvers for inverse problems in seismic data processing and inversion. 
  My work supports applications such as <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">traditional marine seismic acquisition</span> and 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">offshore windfarm site assessments</span>.
</p>

<p>
  At <a href="https://www.shearwatergeo.com/" style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-style: dotted; text-decoration-color: rgba(0,0,0,0.3); font-weight: 600;">Shearwater GeoServices</a>, 
  I lead and support research efforts in 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">self-supervised, physics-informed learning</span>, 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">generative AI for seismic data reconstruction</span>, and 
  <span style="color:#0055aa; font-weight:500;">marine vibroseis technologies</span>. 
  These initiatives are part of our broader mission to advance seismic imaging and acquisition technologies. 
  My long-term goal is to build a <strong>physics-aware deep learning framework</strong> that's scientifically rigorous and accelerates innovation across the energy sector.
</p>


<p>
  Beyond technical research, I'm actively involved in the geoscience community. I currently serve as 
  <strong>Vice President</strong> of the 
  <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/eage-local-chapter-london-450713139/" style="text-decoration: underline dotted; color:#333; font-weight: 600;">EAGE Local Chapter London</a> 
  and as a committee member of the 
  <a href="https://eage.org/communities/young-professionals/" style="text-decoration: underline dotted; color:#333; font-weight: 600;">EAGE Young Professionals</a>. 
  Through these roles, I help promote energy innovation, tackle scientific challenges during the energy transition, and support the next generation of geoscientists.
</p>
